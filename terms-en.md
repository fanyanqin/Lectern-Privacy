## 2. What the app does

Lectern opens PDF documents, keeps a shelf of them, remembers where you stopped reading, and displays pages in one column, two pages side by side, or full screen. It can rotate a single page, and it can search text inside a document and mark the matches.

## 3. What the app does not do

Lectern reads your documents; it does not change them. It cannot annotate, fill a form, redact, or save a modified PDF. There is no cloud storage, no sync between machines, and no account to sign in to. The app has no network permission, so it cannot reach a server even if you want it to.

## 4. Your documents remain yours

Everything you open stays your own file, in its own place on your disk. Lectern never copies a document into its own folder, and never uploads one. Deleting a book from the shelf removes the app's record of it and leaves your file untouched.

## 5. Backups are your responsibility

Lectern does not back up your documents, and cannot restore them. If a file is moved, renamed, corrupted, or deleted outside the app, the shelf entry may stop opening. Keep your own backups. Time Machine covers the app's shelf file; it does not cover documents you placed outside your usual folders.
